# Governance

Context Onion is currently a maintainer-led open project. The initial maintainer is Orlando Garcia.

The maintainer is responsible for reviewing contributions, maintaining coherence across RFCs and project documentation, and deciding when a Draft RFC has enough discussion and evidence to change status.

Contributors are encouraged to propose changes and provide feedback through issues and pull requests in the [canonical repository](https://github.com/contextonion/contextonion). Significant decisions should remain visible in RFCs, issues, or pull-request history.

This project does not currently define a committee, formal voting system, or organizational affiliation. Governance should remain lightweight and may evolve transparently as participation and evidence warrant.
